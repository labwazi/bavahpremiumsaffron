Everything You Need to Know.

Where does Bavah Premium Safron come from?

Bavah Premium Safron is imported from India.

Is your saffron wet or dry?

Our saffron is supplied in dry form, not wet.

Why does original saffron have yellow colour?

The saffron flower contains different parts. Natural saffron can have yellow portions alongside its red threads. Colour alone does not prove authenticity or grade.

Can I cook biryani with Bavah Premium Safron?

Yes. Saffron can be used in biryani, rice, cooking and desserts to provide natural colour, aroma and flavour.
