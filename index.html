Frequently Asked Questions

Is your saffron from India?

Yes. Zafran Gold offers Indian-origin saffron.

Is the saffron wet or dry?

Our product is supplied in naturally dried form, not wet saffron.

Why does saffron contain yellow portions?

The saffron flower has different parts. Natural saffron threads can include yellow portions as well as red portions. Appearance alone does not establish quality.

Can I use it for biryani?

Yes. Saffron is suitable for biryani, rice, cooking, desserts and other food preparations. It provides natural colour and a distinctive aroma.
